Mindset Shift

Conflict Is Not the Problem

Conflict usually means: people care, there are different perspectives, and something important is at stake. From organizational behavior: healthy conflict is task conflict (ideas, approaches); unhealthy conflict is relationship conflict (ego, personal attacks). Your goal is to encourage task conflict and prevent relationship conflict.

“You're not avoiding conflict — you're postponing it at a higher cost.”
