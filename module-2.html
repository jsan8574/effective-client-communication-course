Strategic Communication Techniques

Now that you know the three communication levels, the next question is: how do you actually structure a real conversation? These four techniques give you the words.

Technique 1

BLUF — Bottom Line Up Front

Without BLUF, clients are left guessing at your conclusion:

“So this week we worked on 1,320 accounts, productivity improved, QA was strong, but AR increased slightly…” — the client is thinking: “Is this good or bad?”

Lead with the takeaway. Support it with detail afterward — never the other way around.

Technique 2

Storytelling for Data

Present insights as a compelling narrative: Problem → Insight → Action → Impact.

Technique 3

Frame, Anchor, Explain

Set the overall context first, highlight progress already made, then discuss challenges and next actions.

Frame

Set the overall context first.
“Overall, our trip went really well.”

Anchor

Highlight progress or success already made.
“We managed to visit most of the places we planned.”

Explain

Then discuss the challenge and the adjustment.
“However, we missed one attraction because of heavy traffic, so we adjusted the schedule for the next day.”
